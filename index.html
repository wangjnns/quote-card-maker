FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. All 8 themes, 3 formats and PNG export are free. A tiny watermark sits in the corner — Pro removes it and unlocks premium themes.

Where are my quotes stored?

Nowhere. QuoteCard is a static page — your text and avatar stay in your browser tab and disappear when you close it.

Absolutely. The images you export are yours — use them in newsletters, decks, posts or client work.
